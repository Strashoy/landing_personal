Dado Dice Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Dado Dice is a 3D dice app for tabletop role-playing games by Rolesencia, published on Google Play by Biselbit. Data controller: Lucas Eliel Sosa. This policy explains what data the app uses and what it does with it.

Summary: Dado Dice works without an account and offline. It has no ads, uses no analytics tools, and does not send your data to Rolesencia's servers.

1. Data that stays on your phone

The app stores your roll history, saved rolls, the skins you create (including any images you pick for them) and your preferences on your phone. We don't send them anywhere. If Android backup is turned on, the system may include them in your Google backup; the Discord webhook URL is always excluded from that backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. Discord (optional)

If you turn it on and paste a Discord channel webhook URL, the app posts each roll to that channel: the notation, the result, the game system, the time, the name you choose to display and, if you enable it, an image or animation of the dice generated by the app. It is sent directly from your phone to Discord, encrypted (HTTPS). What gets posted stays in that channel and is subject to Discord's Privacy Policy. Rolesencia does not receive or store those messages. Hidden rolls and rolls with hand-picked values are never posted. You can turn Discord off or remove the webhook at any time.

3. Purchases

The Pro version is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its Privacy Policy; the app only receives confirmation that Pro has been purchased. We never see your payment details.

4. Phone features

  • Vibration: to feel the dice hit.
  • Accelerometer: only if you turn on "Shake to roll", and only while the app is open. Nothing is stored.
  • Text-to-speech: if you turn on "Read the result aloud", the result is passed to your phone's speech engine.
  • Photo picker: only if you choose images for a skin. The app only sees the images you select.

5. Children

Dado Dice is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated with the new date.
